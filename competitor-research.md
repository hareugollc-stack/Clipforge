## Top 5 features worth copying (ranked by impact)

1. **Per-clip virality/engagement scoring.** The signature feature of the category leader and the #1 triage tool as output volume grows. Independent testing shows every tool inflates scores (everything lands 80–99), so *honest* scoring — including a "skip this clip" signal no competitor offers — would be a genuine differentiator.
2. **Smart 9:16 reframing with face/active-speaker tracking.** The crop is where cheap tools visibly fail (sliced faces, drift, bleeding across split-screens). Smooth tracked pans (Klap) and panel-aware cropping (OpusClip) are the bar; this is the most visible quality gap between amateur and pro output.
3. **Word-level animated captions with style presets.** Captions are the single most visible quality signal on a short — Submagic owns this lane (30+ styles, Hormozi presets, emoji triggers). A new tool needs at least a strong preset library here, not just burned-in subtitles.
4. **Filler-word/silence removal + edit-by-transcript.** Makes AI clips feel professionally cut instead of raw excerpts. Text-based trimming (delete a word → video trims) is the fastest-feeling edit interaction in the category.
5. **Paste-a-link → scheduled posts.** URL ingestion (YouTube/Twitch) plus one-click scheduling to TikTok/Reels/Shorts closes the loop from "clip" to "published." Ssemble even auto-clips new uploads from a watched channel — the closest thing to hands-free in the category.

Honorable mentions: AI B-roll insertion, AI hook/title/hashtag generation, brand kits, public API on entry tiers (Ssemble does this; OpusClip gates it behind custom pricing).

## Top 3 complaints to avoid

1. **Punishing metering.** The most hated pattern: credits charged at upload before any output exists, credits that expire, and paywalls that appear *after* the user uploaded and waited (Vizard's "30 clips found, 10 unlocked"; Klap's generate-step paywall). Charge for delivered value — or be radically transparent about the meter up front.
2. **Fake "free" tiers.** "Try for free, no card required" that blocks at generate/download (Klap, Ssemble) breeds 1-star reviews and distrust. Either ship a real free tier or don't claim one.
3. **Billing friction.** Surprise annual charges, hard-to-find cancel buttons, and support that vanishes on billing disputes (Submagic, OpusClip, Klap all carry these complaints on Trustpilot/G2). Easy cancel + clear receipts is a feature in this category — Vizard's 4.7 Trustpilot is built as much on billing trust as on product.

## Notes for the build

- Credit model choice matters: per-source-minute (OpusClip/Vizard) punishes long videos; per-clip (Klap) punishes high clip counts; per-video-of-20-min (Ssemble) is the most generous. Pick the model that matches the target user's footage.
- Nobody does dubbing/voice translation well here except Submagic (Pro) — a gap if targeting multilingual creators.
- Speed is a selling point: Klap's "~4 minutes for a long video" is marketing gold; processing latency should be a headline metric.
